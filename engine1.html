Dbrain Engine-1

Dbrain Engine-1 is an internal data-consolidation tool operated by White Peak Industrial Corp. It connects to White Peak's own QuickBooks Online company, with authorization, to support internal reporting, inventory and reorder tracking, shipment tracking, and sales-tax preparation.

This application is for White Peak's internal use only and is not offered to the public. Data is stored locally and is never sold or shared with third parties.

Connecting QuickBooks

Authorization is performed through Intuit's secure OAuth flow. Access can be revoked at any time from your Intuit account under Settings → Connected Apps.
