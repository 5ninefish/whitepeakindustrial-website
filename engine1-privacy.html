Dbrain Engine-1 — Application Privacy Policy

Internal data application operated by White Peak Industrial Corp. Effective 2026-05-31.

Who this covers

Dbrain Engine-1 ("the Application") is an internal data-consolidation tool operated solely by White Peak Industrial Corp. It is not offered to the public. It accesses White Peak's own business data with White Peak's authorization.

What data we access

With explicit authorization through Intuit's OAuth flow, the Application reads accounting data from White Peak's QuickBooks Online company — vendors, items, purchase orders, bills, invoices, and payments. It also consolidates White Peak's own internal records (order spreadsheets, shipping correspondence, estimates, and packing documents).

How data is used

Data is used only for White Peak's internal reporting and operations: supplier-spend analysis, inventory and reorder tracking, shipment tracking, and sales-tax preparation. Data is not sold, rented, or shared with any third party for marketing or any other purpose.

Where data is stored

Data is stored locally on White Peak's own systems. Access credentials (OAuth tokens) are stored in restricted local files and are never transmitted to third parties other than Intuit, solely to refresh authorized access.

Retention & revocation

Data is retained only as long as needed for White Peak's operations. Access can be revoked at any time from the Intuit account (Settings → Connected Apps), which immediately stops further data access. Locally stored data can be deleted on request.

QuickBooks / Intuit data

Use of QuickBooks Online data is subject to Intuit's terms. The Application accesses only authorized scopes and uses Intuit data solely for the internal purposes described above.
