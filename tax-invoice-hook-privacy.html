Tax Invoice Hook — Privacy

Tax Invoice Hook requests Google authorization to read and update Google Sheets used for invoice tracking. Related document workflows may request read-only access to Google Drive to retrieve and export selected documents. The Google authorization screen identifies the permissions requested for each workflow.

Google credentials and retrieved business records are stored on operator-controlled computers. Sheet updates are transmitted to Google. Exported documents and invoice records are used for authorized business recordkeeping and may be processed by other services the operator separately chooses; this policy does not authorize unrelated disclosure.

Google user data is not sold or used for advertising. Access and use are limited to the operator-requested document and invoice workflows. Use of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

The operator can revoke Google account authorization at any time and delete locally stored credentials and exports. Revoking authorization does not itself delete existing business records or copies retained for business recordkeeping.
