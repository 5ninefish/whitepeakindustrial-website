Tax Invoice Hook — Terms of use

Tax Invoice Hook is a private business tool for authorized White Peak Industrial Corp operators. It is not offered as a public service.

Use is limited to records and Google accounts the operator is authorized to access. The operator reviews invoice and tax records for accuracy and controls any further use or sharing.

Google access can be revoked through the Google account settings. Questions: info@whitepeakindustrial.com.
